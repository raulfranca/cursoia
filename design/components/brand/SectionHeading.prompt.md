# SectionHeading

Opens a page section: mono eyebrow, display title, lead paragraph.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="O programa" title="Quatro semanas, uma prática nova" lead="Cada semana entrega um material que você usa na segunda-feira seguinte." />
```

`align="center"` for landing hero/section openers; left for content pages and emails. The eyebrow is terracotta mono uppercase and stays under ~4 words.
